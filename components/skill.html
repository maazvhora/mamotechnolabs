Frequently Asked Questions

Is the certificate free?

Yes, our standard digital certificates are completely free upon successfully passing the quiz assessment.

What is the certification fee for the assessment?

Most basic quizzes are free. Advanced professional certifications may have a nominal fee starting at ₹99.

How long is the quiz?

Quizzes typically take around 15 to 30 minutes to complete, consisting of multiple-choice and practical skill questions.

Can I retake the quiz?

Yes, if you do not pass on your first attempt, you can retake the quiz after a brief cooling period.

Is login required?

Yes, a quick sign-up or login is required so we can properly generate and save your official certificate.

Who can enroll in these quizzes?

Students, job seekers, working professionals, and career switchers can all take part in our skill assessments.

Do I need prior experience?

We offer quizzes across various levels, from beginner to advanced, so prior experience is not mandatory for foundational tests.

How do I get support if I have doubts?

You can reach out to our support team via email or WhatsApp anytime using the contact details provided in the navigation bar.
